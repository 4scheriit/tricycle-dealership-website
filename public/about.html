Our Story

TriCycle Paradise began as a single midnight build in a Calgary garage: one frame, three borrowed wheels, and a promise to make trikes feel anything but childish.

Friends asked for “whatever that thing is, but in my size.” Weekends disappeared into welding, tuning, and test rides down quiet side streets.

What We Believe

By 2021, those garage builds had turned into a small riders club that met for sunrise coffee loops along the river.

The belief was simple: stability should never feel slow. Three wheels could be confident, fast, and unapologetically fun.

The Flagship

In 2023 the club needed a home. The flagship showroom opened with performance, cargo, electric, and family models—all tuned on the same streets they were built to ride.

Fitting sessions feel more like fittings for a tailored suit than a bike sale: sizing, posture, and finish all dialed in.

The Team

Today our crew blends mechanics, product designers, and lifelong riders who all share the same obsession: the perfect three-wheel carve around a corner.

Every new model starts in the sketchbook, moves to the workshop, and finishes its prototype lap on Calgary pavement—never just on a spreadsheet.
